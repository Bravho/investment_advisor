## Tax Considerations

### Thai Dividend Withholding Tax (WHT)
- **Rate:** 10% WHT on dividends paid by Thai companies to non-Thai residents
- **For Thai residents:** Dividend tax credit available (can choose between 10% WHT credit or including in taxable income with full tax credit)
- **Recommendation:** Consult Thai tax advisor for individual circumstances

### US Tax (for US persons)
- Thai dividends are foreign qualified dividends (subject to verification with tax advisor)
- 10% Thai WHT may be creditable against US tax (Form 1116 foreign tax credit)
- FATCA/FBAR reporting required if aggregate foreign accounts >$10,000

**Disclaimer:** Tax information is general only. Consult qualified tax professionals in Thailand and home jurisdiction.
