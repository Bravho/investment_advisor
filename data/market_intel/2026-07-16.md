## 1. Headline / Regime Read — Hormuz toll DROPPED, conflict continues

The single most important change since the Jul-15 run: **Trump abandoned the 20% Strait of Hormuz cargo toll** (CNBC, Jul-14). The *economic-friction* leg of the Jul-13 escalation is now OFF — but **US airstrikes and the naval blockade continue** into a third day, and Hormuz crossings remain down >50% w/w (57 transits Fri–Sun vs the prior week). This is a **partial walk-back, not a de-escalation**: the shooting hasn't stopped, only the tariff on transit was removed.

Market response: **Brent eased**, settling **~$84.73** (Jul-14; September future ~$85.92, still the highest since Jun-15) — vs the ~$86–88 the Jul-15 model assumed. So the realised oil regime is a touch **lower and less frictional** than 24h ago.
