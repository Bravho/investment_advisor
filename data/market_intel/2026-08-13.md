### The headline

**Buying SCBGOLD costs more than owning it for a year.** The 0.535% entry charge is
**1.227 years** of the 0.436025% management fee. For a 3-month-to-2-year holder the
fee that matters is the one paid on day one, not the TER.

Per THB 100,000, all-in known cost:

| Hold | Total cost | Annualised |
|---|---|---|
| 3 months | THB 802 (0.80%) | **3.21%** |
| 24 months | THB 1,621 (1.62%) | **0.81%** |

**Holding period alone swings the annualised fee 4x.** That is a direct, quantified
argument for the long end of Tho's own mandate on this position — and against
incremental top-ups, since each new purchase pays the full 0.535% again.

Three caveats, all of which push the true cost **up**:

1. Every total above is a **lower bound**. The registrar fee is published as "as
   announced" and other fees are "actual expenses" — neither is a number.
2. SCBGOLD is a **feeder into SPDR Gold Trust**. GLD's own sponsor fee (~0.40% p.a.)
   is borne inside the master and appears nowhere in this schedule.
3. The waivers are **waivers, not abolitions**. The prospectus permits 0.535%
   back-end and 0.535% switching-out. Permitted maxima total 1.287% p.a. against
   0.468% charged — **2.75x headroom** that can be used without a prospectus change.

### And the sleeve is unhedged

SCBAM runs a THB-hedged class, **SCBGOLDH**. Tho holds the unhedged **SCBGOLD**, so
the position is a joint bet on USD gold *and* USD/THB. Switching classes costs 0.535%
in, 0% out — about 1.23 years of management fee. With the BOT policy rate at 1.00%
against a much higher US rate, hedging THB/USD **costs carry**; it does not earn it.
No recommendation is made, deliberately — see §4.

## 4. ⚠️ Finding: a signal that reverses every session is not a signal

The FX sign has now flipped in **three consecutive runs**:

- 11 Aug: baht strengthening → gold sleeve headwind
- 12 Aug: baht weakening → *"the sign has flipped and gold commentary should be read accordingly"*
- 13 Aug: baht strengthening → headwind again

Each run faithfully recorded the sign and each run reversed its predecessor. The
vendor noise alone (33.082 tonight vs 33.1160 recorded last night for an adjacent
session, from different vendors) is a meaningful fraction of the moves being narrated.

**Adopted rule: single-session FX moves are not to be given a portfolio interpretation.**
A hedging decision on this position costs 0.535% — roughly 1.23 years of management
fee, and roughly 6x the largest daily FX move in the sample. Acting on a weekly-
reversing signal at that toll is noise-trading with a tax. `gold_fee_drag.py`
therefore returns `recommendation: None` for the hedged/unhedged question by design,
with the reason stated in the code rather than left implicit.

This joins the standing read-path hazards: the Buddhist-era trap (2569 = 2026), the
futures/spot trap, and the 12 August rule that *a retrieved figure with no explicit
in-text date is not a figure*.
