**Why this went unnoticed for three weeks.** A dead daemon and a broken parser are
indistinguishable from downstream: in both cases the last price simply stops advancing.

like a fresh one, and a zero daily delta read as "the market was flat" when it actually meant
"nobody asked the market." The Aug-07 run even noted a SET index low of 1,607.51 and foreign
outflows — direct evidence the market had moved — while the book reported zero change, and
still concluded the retrieval path was at fault.

from stale notes.

right; the label on the number was inverted. Corrected here so it is not propagated further.
