## 1. 🚨 The carried price marks were wrong, not just stale

For six consecutive runs this project has carried SET equity marks forward from
2026-08-04 while the live collector stayed down, and has said so honestly each
time. Tonight those marks were checked against the market for the first time.

| Ticker | Carried mark | Actual 2026-08-07 close | Error |
|---|---|---|---|
| BAFS.BK | 9.80 | **11.60** | **+18.37%** |
| PTTEP.BK | 145.00 | **149.00** | +2.76% |
| SCB.BK | 151.50 | **153.50** | +1.32% |

The book, ex-gold, is worth **THB 634,620**, not the THB 593,460 reported for five
consecutive nights. The understatement is **THB 41,160 (+6.94%)**.

The BAFS number is the serious one. 9.80 did not correspond to 2026-08-04 in any
sense — the real close that day was 10.80. BAFS last changed hands near 9.80 in
**late February**, and again near 9.75 on **17 June**. A mark of unknown vintage
was being stamped with a specific recent date, which made it impossible to
falsify. Meanwhile BAFS ran from 8.75 on 29 May to 11.60 on 7 August — **+32.6%**,
on a position that is a third of Tho's equity book — and the entire move was
invisible to this system.

This is the same failure family as `avg_cost_price = 0` and the dividend store that
was never written: **a value that is absent or unverified renders downstream as a
perfectly normal number.** The specific new rule is that *a carried mark must carry
the date it was observed, never the date it was copied.*

What this invalidates: every position value, total, weight and concentration
percentage in the 2026-08-05 through 2026-08-09 snapshots, including the
repeatedly-reported finding that BAFS sat at 30.05% of the book. It was ~33%.

**Remedy shipped tonight:** `data_collector/th_price_fallback.py`, an independent
end-of-day SET price path that does not require the daemon, the database or
yfinance. See §7.

## 3. Correction: BAFS has *not* reported H1-2026

The Aug-09 run recorded that BAFS reported around 2026-08-06, that the print was
missed, and that retrieving it was the highest-value outstanding data task. **That
was wrong and is retracted.**

The claim traces to a Thai-language article reporting net profit **THB 56m (+29%
YoY)** with a **THB 0.11** interim dividend. Those are **Q2/2568** figures — i.e.
**2025** — with an ex-date of 28 August 2025. The trap is **Buddhist-era year
labelling**: 2568 = 2025, 2569 = 2026. Thai financial sources use BE by default, and
a one-year slip reads as a perfectly plausible current result. This is a standing
hazard for every Thai source this project reads and is now documented in the
`earnings_tracker.py` seed note.

Verified two ways: BAFS IR "Financial Highlights" still headlines **Q1/2026** as the
latest published period, and the Q2 column for FY2569 in the quarterly grid is
empty. The original **~14 August** estimate is consistent with the 2025 pattern
(board approval mid-August, XD Aug-28) and is retained.

**Last published period — Q1/2026, for reference when the H1 print lands:**

- Total revenue THB 991.01m (core 967.0m), net profit **THB 151.8m (+6% YoY)**,
  net margin 14%, EBITDA THB 532.9m (+2%)
- **Aviation refuelling volume +5% YoY, at 92% of pre-COVID** — this is the number
  that matters; judge the H1 print on throughput, not headline margin
- Pipeline transport volume **−8%**, on tight market supply
- Foreign tourist arrivals **−2% YoY at 9.3m** — a headwind BAFS grew through
- Total assets THB 21,936.8m, liabilities THB 15,466.9m, interest-bearing D/E 2.0x
- Fitch Thailand affirmed **BBB(tha), Stable**
- Northern pipeline phase 3 (Ang Thong–Saraburi) under construction, commercial

different trades, and the difference matters for both the gold sleeve and the baht.

of 10 August.

## 7. Improvements shipped tonight

**`data_collector/th_price_fallback.py` (new).** An independent end-of-day SET price
reader — no daemon, no PostgreSQL, no yfinance, standard library only. Provides
`fetch_daily_history()`, `latest_close()`, `latest_closes()`, `portfolio_marks()`,
`save_quotes()` and `self_test()`. Design rules carried over from this project's three
prior silent-failure defects:

- every accessor returns `None` on failure, never `0` and never a carried value
- every quote carries the as-of date parsed from its own source row; an undated row
  is dropped, not guessed
- `latest_close()` **refuses** a quote older than `max_age_days` rather than warning —
  the fallback must not become its own stale feed
- `portfolio_marks()` returns `total_thb = None` if *any* leg is unpriced, because a
  partial sum understates a book while reading as a real number
- Buddhist-era dates are converted explicitly (`2569 → 2026`), the trap that caused
  tonight's false earnings alarm
- `self_test()` exercises the real parse against live HTML, per the standing rule to
  verify at the read path rather than the write path

**`portfolio_engine/earnings_tracker.py` (edited).** The BAFS seed note now carries the
false-alarm retraction, the Buddhist-era explanation, both verification points, and an
instruction not to re-open the chase. No existing code path was altered.

**Data written:** `data/snapshots/2026-08-10.json` (corrected marks + full correction
record), `data/watchlist.json` (refreshed), this file.
