## 1. Headline — First De-escalation Tell in the Oil War

After four straight weekly gains, the one-way escalation broke on **Jul-24: Brent settled DOWN 3.9% at ~$96.78** (WTI $89.31) on reports that **China is pushing — with Pakistan mediating — a resumption of US-Iran talks** centred on the Strait of Hormuz. China's and Pakistan's foreign ministers jointly urged a ceasefire on Jul-17; Pakistan and Iran are now "exploring a path" to restart talks, though Pakistan flagged a **precondition not yet met** — a halt to attacks on Saudi/Gulf targets.

This is a **diplomatic tell, not a resolution.** US strikes continue and all three physical supply disruptions persist:
- **Hormuz** crossings constrained (down to a single tanker at the week's low);
- **Houthi Red Sea / Saudi-port blockade** — the realised second front;
- **Kazakhstan's CPC Black Sea terminal offline** after four drone strikes in four days; Kazakhstan **cut crude output**. CPC carries ~80% of Kazakh exports and >1% of global supply.

Net: the **fear premium eased off the top** while the barrels stay physically off the market.
