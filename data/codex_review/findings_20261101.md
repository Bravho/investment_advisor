## Top five improvements

1. **Centralize mandatory advice invariants.** One guard should enforce owner holds, locked funds, freshness, unknown handling and no-order semantics across every recommendation path.  
   **Expected benefit:** eliminates contradictory advice and makes policy violations testable.

2. **Turn P3 into an executable candidate-vs-baseline gate.** Cover all horizons, exact live feature code, weights and rebalance actions; fail CI/job execution when the stated mean-skill/hit rule is not met.  
   **Expected benefit:** prevents unvalidated changes from quietly entering production.

3. **Add a portfolio-wide data-quality contract.** Every value should carry source, as-of date, age, proxy status and coverage; stale or missing inputs should propagate as unknown.  
   **Expected benefit:** prevents false precision in forecasts, VaR, sizing and rebalancing.

4. **Make portfolio adjustment advice cash-feasible and invariant-driven.** Preserve current positions for classes inside bands, include actual cash, constrain total buys to funding, and test no-trade/no-sell invariants.  
   **Expected benefit:** produces implementable adjustment plans rather than disconnected target trades.

5. **Improve risk and forecast validation with non-overlapping, point-in-time samples.** Evaluate exact target sessions, include costs/benchmark returns, report uncertainty by market and horizon, and require adequate portfolio-weight coverage.  
   **Expected benefit:** more credible skill estimates, better calibrated ranges, and less understated portfolio risk.
